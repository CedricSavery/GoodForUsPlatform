PCI/DSS COMPLIANT ARCHITECTURE

GoodForUs utilizes industry-leading encryption and secure gateway protocols to protect every contribution.

1. PCI/DSS Compliance

SolutionsAe Inc. maintains strict adherence to the **Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI/DSS)**. Our platform is engineered to ensure that sensitive cardholder data is never stored directly on our servers. All transaction data is tokenized and transmitted via encrypted channels to Tier-1 financial processors.

2. Data Encryption

All traffic to and from the GoodForUs suite is protected by **256-bit SSL/TLS encryption**. This ensures that campaign manager data, donor information, and proprietary campaign logic remain private and secure during transit.

3. Secure Campaign Logic

Our proprietary fundraising logic is hosted within a high-security environment. Each campaign builder—P2P Pro, Crowdfund, and Multi-Step—is sandboxed to prevent cross-site contamination and unauthorized data access.

4. Automated Fiscal Protection

The automated PDF receipting system is governed by secure document generation protocols. Each receipt contains a unique **Identifier (ID)** linked to the SolutionsAe fintech gateway, ensuring the authenticity of every transaction and preventing fraudulent documentation.

5. Access Control

Campaign Managers are granted access via multi-factor authentication protocols. We monitor system logs 24/7 for unauthorized access attempts or suspicious behavioral patterns within our fundraising modules.

6. Security Audits

SolutionsAe Inc. conducts regular internal security audits and vulnerability assessments. Our commitment to security ensures that our proprietary fintech remains at the forefront of philanthropic safety.
